# What we need from you to launch the SRN website

Hi Fortune,

The website is being built now. Everything on this list is something only you can provide — we can build every page without it, but **we cannot launch without it.** Right now the site is running on obvious placeholder content, which is deliberate: it means nothing fake can accidentally go live.

Please send whatever you have as you get it, rather than waiting to have everything. Photos first if you can — they matter most.

**Send to:** [PLACEHOLDER: agree a shared Drive folder or email]

---

## 1. Photographs — the most important item

Real photos of real SRN training are what will make this site credible. A visitor decides in about five seconds whether an organisation is substantial, and photography does most of that work. This is the single biggest difference between a site that looks established and one that looks new.

**Please don't buy stock photos.** A generic photo of strangers in a meeting room reads as fake immediately, and for an evidence organisation that is a particularly bad look. Real photos taken on a phone are far better than polished stock.

| What                              | How many                        | What it needs to be                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| --------------------------------- | ------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Main photo for the front page** | 1                               | A wide (landscape) photo of an actual SRN training session. People doing something — talking, working, presenting — not lined up smiling at the camera. As large a file as you have; at least 2400 pixels across. |
| **Other event photos**            | 4–6                             | Workshops, presentations, group shots. **Zoom screenshots are fine** — a grid of participants from an online session works well. At least 1600 pixels across.                                                     |
| **Photos of people**              | Every person listed on the site | One per team member. Square if possible, at least 800 pixels. Different backgrounds and lighting are fine — the site treats them consistently.                                                                    |

**If you're unsure whether a photo is good enough, send it anyway.** It's easier for us to say "this one works" than for you to guess.

**One thing we need in writing:** confirmation that the people in these photos are happy to appear on a public website. A short note saying so is enough.

## 2. Your logo — the original file

We need the **original design file** for the SRN logo, not a screenshot or a photo of it.

Look for a file ending in `.svg`, `.ai`, `.eps`, or `.pdf`. Whoever designed the logo will have this. A JPG or PNG will look blurry when enlarged and we can't pull the exact brand colours from it.

If you genuinely can't find the original, tell us and we'll work from what you have — but please check with the designer first.

---

## 3. Your numbers

The front page shows 4–6 large figures — the kind of thing that immediately tells a visitor the scale of what SRN does. For example:

- Researchers trained
- Workshops or webinars delivered
- Countries reached
- Institutions partnered with
- Members or community size

**Only send numbers you would be comfortable defending publicly.** If a figure is approximate, say so and we'll present it as "500+" rather than a precise-looking claim. For an evidence organisation, an exaggerated number is a genuine risk, so we'd rather have a smaller true figure.

Please also tell us **what period each number covers** (for example, "since 2022").

---

## 4. Countries

The site includes a map of the countries SRN has reached. Please send the full list.

We know about: Nigeria, Rwanda, Ghana, Cameroon, Pakistan, Brazil. Please correct anything wrong and add what's missing.

---

## 5. Your programmes

For **each** programme — Beginner Academy, Practical Course, Mentorship Programme, Webinar Series, and Institutional Training — we need:

- **Who it's for** (in plain terms: "postgraduate students starting their first review")
- **How it runs** — online, in person, or both
- **How long** it lasts
- **What people can do afterwards** that they couldn't before — three or four points
- **What happens when someone is interested** — do they register, apply, or contact you?
- **The cost**, and whether there are discounted or free places (see section 8)

If a programme has run before, roughly how many people have taken it?

---

## 6. Quotes from people

Two or three short quotes from people who've been through SRN training — a sentence or two each. Specific beats general: "I completed my first systematic review six months after the course" is far stronger than "the training was excellent."

For each: their **name**, **role**, **institution**, and ideally a **photo**.

**We need their permission to publish this.** A forwarded email where they say yes is fine.

---

## 7. Partner organisations

Logos for any organisation SRN works with or is supported by — AuthorAID/INASP, partner universities, funders.

Same as the SRN logo: the original file rather than a screenshot, if you have it. Also confirm you're permitted to display each one; some organisations have rules about this.

---

## 8. Payments — please confirm the details

You've confirmed courses are paid, so we're building payment into the site using **Paystack**. To finish this we need:

- **The price of each paid programme**, and which currency
- **Which programmes are free** (if any)
- **Are there discounted or sponsored places?** For example reduced fees for students, or for particular countries
- **What happens if someone cancels?** Do you refund, and up to when?
- **Your Paystack account details** — we'll send separate instructions for connecting it securely. Please don't email us the keys.
- **For donations:** is there a suggested amount, or does the donor decide?

**One thing to check now, because it takes time:** if you want to charge in **US dollars** as well as naira, Paystack has to approve that on your account. It isn't switched on by default. Please start that request early — we can build everything else while it's pending.

---

## 9. Written content

We're rewriting all the text rather than copying from the old site, but we need the facts right. Please confirm:

- **The SRN story** — how it started, when, the connection to ACSRM and AuthorAID, why it was founded
- **What SRN is trying to achieve** — mission and vision, in your own words
- **Every team member**: full name, role, institution, and whether they want their LinkedIn or ORCID linked
- **Contact details** — the email address for enquiries, and which social media accounts are current
- **Any documents** you want available for download, such as annual reports

---

## 10. Small things we'll need eventually

- The web address you want (for example `systematicreviews.org`) and who currently controls it
- Which staff need logins to edit the site, and their email addresses
- Whether SRN has an existing privacy policy, or whether we should draft one

---

## What happens next

Once we have the photos, logo, numbers, and programme details, we can put the real site together. The rest can follow.

**The launch check is simple:** the site cannot go live while any placeholder content remains. Every item above is part of that check.

If anything here is unclear or seems like more than you have, tell us — there's almost always a sensible alternative, and it's better to know early.
